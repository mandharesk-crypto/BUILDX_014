Head & Spine Stabilization

  • Do not move the victim's head or neck under any circumstance.
  • Hold head steady with both hands on either side of temples.
  • If wearing a helmet, leave it on unless airway is choked.

STEP 3

Unconscious Airway Clearance

  • If unconscious but breathing, turn to side recovery position.
  • Gently clear mouth fluids using a clean handkerchief.
  • Keep chin tilted forward so tongue does not block throat.
⚠️ Keeps airway open to prevent choking & suffocation.
STEP 4

Compound Limb Splinting

  • Do not attempt to push protruding bones back into flesh.
  • Immobilize the limb in position found with rolled cloth.
  • Check distal pulse to ensure circulation is not choked.
⚠️ Prevents vascular tearing from jagged bone edges.
